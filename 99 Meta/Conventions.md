# Conventions
- Dates: ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Daily notes: `01 Daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Weekly: `02 Weekly/YYYY-Www.md`.
- Every note (except README, AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md) has `type` in frontmatter: daily, weekly, project, area, resource, meeting, inbox, memory, prompt, decision, agent-session, memory, runbook, agent-instructions, meta.
- Tags: lowercase, hyphenated. `#example` marks demo content.
- `source: agent` on anything an AI wrote.
